A new industry-integrated learning experience is set to take shape at Noida Institute of Engineering and Technology (NIET), Greater Noida.

The upcoming Capgemini CodeXperience Centre at NIET is envisioned as a learning environment designed to bring students closer to the way technology professionals learn, collaborate, solve problems, and build solutions in the corporate world.

By bringing together technical learning, hands-on practice, collaborative problem-solving, and industry exposure, the Centre aims to help students connect academic knowledge with its application in professional technology environments.

CAPGEMINI NIET

Bringing Industry and Academia Closer

The upcoming Capgemini CodeXperience Centre at NIET marks another step towards strengthening industry-academia engagement on campus.

The Centre is envisioned to combine Capgemini's industry perspective with NIET's academic ecosystem, creating opportunities for students to understand how technical knowledge can translate into professional application.

It is designed to encourage students to move beyond knowing concepts to applying, collaborating, building and experiencing how technology-driven work happens.
